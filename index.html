Elegance woven with heritage

At Axom Decor Thread, we celebrate the richness of Assamese textile heritage through a carefully curated collection of handwoven sarees, stoles, and textiles. We work closely with skilled weaving communities to bring together pieces that embody exceptional craftsmanship, cultural authenticity, and timeless design.

Each piece is selected for its quality, artistry, and connection to tradition. By supporting generations-old weaving practices and presenting them to a wider audience, we help preserve a legacy of craftsmanship while ensuring it remains relevant for contemporary lifestyles.

We believe true luxury lies in authenticity, thoughtful craftsmanship, and the stories woven into every textile.
